Known-good recipe

Woodcut, linocut, letterpress, scratchboard, engraving. A style whose ideal form is already broken is immune to bloom, because there is no clean reference to fall short of. Compose at 948×1659 for a business card, or 576 wide for no resampling at all.

Shape

The printer's built-in look: one monospace face, no layout, wrapped at the head width. For anything you want to keep, use Quote instead.

    Typeface

    Two opposite kinds of face survive bloom: heavy monoline (Arial Black, Courier New Bold), which has nothing thin to eat, and deliberately irregular (Special Elite), where the texture hides the damage. What fails is the refined middle. Weight beats size — Bold at 64 px beat the same face Regular at 96.

    Glyphs are rasterised from the outline at 4× and resolved down, so cutoff 128 is the outline exactly: a dot inks when the outline covers more than half of it. Bolden widens every stem geometrically instead, which is the better tool below about 40 px. Try 0.5–1 on slab and serif faces.
